Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Tick Every does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

Data and permissions

The timer runs locally on the Pebble watch. Timer preferences and the selected interface language are stored locally so they can be restored the next time the app opens. Tick Every has no account system, analytics, advertising or location access.

Mobile configuration page

Changing the watch language opens a static HTTPS page hosted on s3.grossholtz.net. The page contains no analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts. The selected language is sent to the watch through the Pebble app. Like most web servers, the host may process standard request metadata such as IP address and user agent for delivery and operational logs.

Third parties

Tick Every does not send timer settings or usage data to the developer or to third parties. Installation, distribution and the configuration bridge are handled by the Pebble platform under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deletion

Uninstalling Tick Every removes the app and its locally stored preferences from the watch.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the “Contact developer” action on the Tick Every Appstore page or email oiawo.cir@gmail.com.
