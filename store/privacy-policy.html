Tick Every

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 August 2026

Tick Every does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

Data and permissions

The timer runs locally on the Pebble watch. Timer preferences, the selected interface language and the optional statistics setting are stored locally so they can be restored the next time the app opens. Tick Every has no account system, analytics, advertising or location access.

Optional session history

Session history is disabled by default. If you enable it, Tick Every stores up to 32 completed sessions on the watch. A record contains its end date and time, total and active durations, cycle count, interval, start delay and whether numbered haptics were enabled. When the history is full, the oldest record is replaced. Sessions stopped before active timing begins, or interrupted without a confirmed stop, are not recorded.

The Pebble mobile component merges each watch snapshot into a separate archive in PebbleKit JS local storage. The watch keeps 32 recent sessions; the phone archive has no session-count limit imposed by Tick Every and is displayed in pages of 32. Its actual capacity depends on the storage quota provided by the phone and Pebble WebView. It is not uploaded to Tick Every, the developer or a statistics service.

If more than 32 sessions are completed without a successful phone synchronization, sessions already evicted from the watch cannot be recovered. A watch reset or empty snapshot does not erase sessions already archived on the phone.

Mobile configuration page

Changing the watch language or statistics setting opens a static HTTPS page hosted on s3.grossholtz.net. The page contains no analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts. Settings are sent to the watch through the Pebble app. History is passed to the page in the URL fragment, which browsers do not send to the hosting server. Like most web servers, the host may process standard request metadata such as IP address and user agent for delivery and operational logs.

Third parties

Tick Every does not send timer settings, session records or usage data to the developer or to third parties. Installation, distribution and the configuration bridge are handled by the Pebble platform under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deletion

Disabling session history stops new records but does not silently erase existing ones. Uninstalling Tick Every removes its preferences and history from the watch, but does not necessarily remove PebbleKit JS data already archived by the mobile app. Removing the Pebble mobile app or clearing its application data removes the local phone archive.
